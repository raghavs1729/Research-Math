Supplements `LITERATURE-2026-11-27.md` (which covers the arXiv listings, OpenAlex/Semantic Scholar citation lists,
GitHub and the trackers as of the morning of 27 Nov). Requested by the parent session: list every arXiv paper by
Kanade, Russell, Tsuchioka, Warnaar posted after arXiv:2404.03851, and every work citing KRTW, and check whether any
proves (ii), (iii) or (iv) for some k ≥ 3. Method: arXiv API (author queries restricted to math categories, filtered by

latest 300 submissions in each of math.CO, math.NT, math.RT, math.QA for keywords (Primc, Capparelli, Andrews–Gordon,
Hall–Littlewood, Rogers–Ramanujan, Kanade, Warnaar, Bailey). Abstracts read for every item; scripts and raw output in the
session scratchpad (not committed).

z = q for k ≥ 3.**
