# OPEN-LIST - conjectural Ramanujan-type series: what was open on 2026-11-24, and what the WZ scan found

This file is the index.  The full survey (sources read, how each status was checked, caveats) is in
`survey/SURVEY-A.md` (1/pi^k families of Guillera, Gourevitch, Cullen, Almkvist-Guillera, ...),
`survey/SURVEY-B.md` (Z.-W. Sun's hypergeometric conjectures, with and without harmonic numbers) and
`survey/SURVEY-C.md` (2023-2026 sweep, Au's "?=" list, other authors).  Every identity listed was recomputed
numerically (`survey/verify_*.py`, outputs `survey/verify_*.out.txt`): 81 + 89 + 63 checks, all >= 65 digits.

**Corrections to the brief.** Au's WZ-seeds paper is arXiv:2312.14051 (not 2209.01731). It already proves
Gourevitch's 1/pi^3 series and the Cullen and Zhao 1/pi^4 series; Guillera (arXiv:2503.00570) and Shalev
(arXiv:2610.04770) gave further proofs. They are **not open** and were used only for calibration.
