## 1. List of defects (most severe first)

**D1 (major, attribution/novelty; not a correctness issue).** Lemma 2 remark: "(For r = 2 this is the rank 2 ↔ rank 3 correspondence attributed to Zwegers … Iterating it is what is new here.)" This understates the prior art.
- Cao–Wang, *Some new modular Nahm sums of ranks 3 and 4*, arXiv:2606.13590 (11 Jun 2026), state exactly Lemma 2 for general rank r and any pair i<j: "It is known that one may construct higher rank Nahm sums from lower rank sums. One useful way to increase the rank of Nahm sums is the following formula (see e.g. [1, p. 20]): 1/((q;q)_i(q;q)_j) = Σ_{k≥0} q^{(i−k)(j−k)}/((q;q)_k(q;q)_{i−k}(q;q)_{j−k}) (1.5). If we pick two variables n_i and n_j (1 ≤ i < j ≤ n) and applying the above formula … then we can rewrite (1.1) as f_{A,B,C}(q) = Σ_{(n_1,…,n_{r+1})} q^{½ ñᵀAñ + Bᵀñ + n_i n_j + C}/((q;q)_{n_1}⋯(q;q)_{n_{r+1}}) (1.6), where ñ = (…, n_i+n_{r+1}, …, n_j+n_{r+1}, …, n_r)". Their rank 3 → 4 "lifting operators" L_1, L_2, L_3 (also arXiv:2508.12468 (1.13)–(1.16)) are Lemma 2 for the three pairs.
- Ekholm–Kucharski–Longhi, *Multi-cover skeins, quivers, and 3d N=2 dualities*, arXiv:1910.06193, §4.5 "Proof of invariance for general quivers: linking", eq. (4.31)–(4.36): for a symmetric quiver with adjacency matrix C = (r k a; k s b; a b c), the "linked" quiver C′ = (r, k+1, a, r+k; k+1, s, b, s+k; a, b, c, a+b; r+k, s+k, a+b, r+s+2k) satisfies P^{Q′}(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)|_{x_4=x_1x_2} = P^Q(x_1,x_2,x_3), where P^Q = Σ_d (−q)^{d·C·d} x^d/∏(q²;q²)_{d_i}. With q² → q this is Lemma 2 verbatim (new row = sum of the two rows, diagonal r+s+2k, off-diagonal raised by 1, x_new = x_a x_b, i.e. B°_0 = B_a+B_b). Iterating linking/unlinking is the subject of Kucharski–Larraguível–Noshchenko–Sułkowski, *Unlinking symmetric quivers*, arXiv:2312.14905, and of Jankowski–Kucharski–Larraguível–Noshchenko–Sułkowski, *Permutohedra for knots and quivers*, arXiv:2105.11806.
- Already in the Nahm-sum literature the lift is used to reach an inverse Cartan matrix: Cao–Wang arXiv:2412.15767 (4.2)–(4.3) and Wang–Wang arXiv:2512.07790 §4 ("C(D_3) can be lifted from the matrix (1, −1/2; −1/2, 3/4)") and Sun–Wang §2(f) ((T1,A3) from Zagier's Example 9).
The document must cite these and restrict its novelty claim to the specific E8/E6 (and D_r, T_{2r+1}) chains. None of these sources contains the E8 or E6 chain (checked by full-text grep of the downloaded texts for E8/E_8/E6/Cartan/Andrews).
