## 1. General formula
Sec. 3.3 "Half-index and modular functions" (\label{subsec: half index}), eq (half-index+loops):
  I_half^{W_Q}(q,nu,eta) = \sum_{m\in N^r} q^{\frac12 m^t K m} eta^{-a^t m} (-q^{1/2})^{-\mu^t m} q^{-Q^t m} / ((q)_{m_1}...(q)_{m_r}) |_{\mu -> \mu_0 + nu a}
A-twist (nu=-1, eta=1), first \label{main formula}:
  \chi_A(q)= q^{\Delta} I_half(q,\nu=-1,\eta=1) = \sum_{m\in N^r} \frac{q^{\frac12 m^t K m+\Delta}(-q^{1/2})^{-(\mu_0-a)^t m}}{(q)_{m_1}\ldots(q)_{m_r}}
with Wilson line (second \label{main formula}):
  q^{\Delta} I^{W_{\vec Q}}_{half}(q,\nu=-1,\eta=1) = \sum_{m\in N^r} \frac{q^{\frac12 m^t K m+\Delta}(-q^{1/2})^{-(\mu_0-a)^t m} q^{-Q^t m}}{(q)_{m_1}\ldots(q)_{m_r}}
"which we denote by q^\Delta I_A[W_Q]".  So I_A[W_Q] = sum WITHOUT q^Delta; sign factor is (-1)^{(mu_0-a).m}; your recorded form is right.
Meaning: K = CS level matrix (positive definite integer); mu_0 = superconformal-R mixing vector, a = axial-symmetry charge vector (A = a.T), both integer vectors listed per example; Q = Wilson-line charge; Delta = modular prefactor (given per example as the q^{..} in front of the character). (-q^{1/2})^{-(mu0-a).m} gives sign (-1)^{(mu0-a).m} and q^{-(mu0-a).m/2}.
Relation to Zagier (Sec. 4.3 "Comparision with Zagier's result"): A=K, B=-(mu_0-a-2Q)/2, C=Delta, valid when B integral.
Logical status, Sec. 4.1 first paragraph: "Through these examples, we confirm the conjecture in the previous section by expressing the $I_A[W_{\vec{Q}}]$ in terms of the known characters of RCFTs. ... The identification of the indices with known RCFT characters are conjectures based on the $q$-series expansion, except for a few cases with known Nahm sum representations."
Search: "all positive definite integer K for r=1,2,3 with entries between -17 and 17". Also (Sec 4.3) Nahm-conjecture-style modularity of the half-index stated only as "is expected to be a modular function" (eq conjecture2). They remark the sign factor means a generalization of Nahm's conjecture.

## 2/3. Examples. Notation: I_A[W_Q] = sum_m (-1)^{(mu0-a).m} q^{m^T K m/2 -(mu0-a).m/2 - Q.m}/prod (q)_{m_i}; verified claim = (I_A = q^{P} * character, P as in paper), exact incl. exponent prefactor, through q^25 (t^50, t=q^{1/2}), my script, exact integers.
Character defs (App. "Characters of rational VOAs"): chi^{M(P,Q)}_{(r,s)} = q^{h-c/24}/(q)_inf sum_n (q^{n^2PQ+n(Qr-Ps)} - q^{(nP+r)(nQ+s)}); this gives for M(2,Q): (1,s) = q^{h-c/24} prod_{n !=0,+-s mod Q}(1-q^n)^{-1} (standard; consistent with all checks). SM: same with exponents/2 and factor (-q^{1/2};q)_inf/(q)_inf. chi_F = q^{-1/48}prod(1+q^{n+1/2}). chi^{U(1)_k}_mu = q^{mu^2/2k-1/24}/(q)_inf sum_m q^{km^2/2+mu m}. W3(3,7): [(q^7;q^7)/(q)]^2 prod_6 (q^{j};q^7) "up to q-prefactor".
Status of each: paper says "conjecture from q-expansion" for all except where noted.
